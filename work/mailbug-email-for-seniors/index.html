Email, redesigned for the people email forgot

— The problem

Mainstream email clients are optimised for power users. For older users — failing eyesight, unsteady hands, unfamiliar gestures — they're a wall of friction. The product brief: an email client a 70-year-old can use confidently in a week.

— Approach
  1. 01

    Rebuilt the inbox around 44px+ hit targets, large type, and high-contrast colour — accessibility-first, not retrofitted.

  2. 02

    Stripped the action surface down to its essentials: read, reply, photo, call. Hid power features behind a single 'more' affordance.

  3. 03

    Voice cues for confirmations — 'Email sent', 'Three new messages' — because reading is harder than hearing for some users.

  4. 04

    Real-time sync over Firebase, GPS-aware sender hints, and a contact list that prioritises family.
