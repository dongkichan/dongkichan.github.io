— The problem

A national tax platform serving citizens at scale: many independent modules (filing, payments, audit, registry, reports, support), many teams, one shared shell. Coupling kills velocity here — but so does fragmentation.

— Approach
  1. 01

    Adopted a micro-frontend architecture: each module independently deployable, integrated through a single shell with shared design tokens and authentication.

  2. 02

    Used NgRx for predictable state across high-traffic flows — citizens don't tolerate 'undefined' on a tax form.

  3. 03

    Reactive forms with strict validation, accessibility baked in, server-driven schema where business rules change quarterly.

  4. 04

    Wrote comprehensive Jasmine unit tests that raised coverage and cut regression incidents — the only sustainable way to ship at this risk profile.

— What I took away

“Micro-frontends are an organisational pattern wearing a technical disguise. The moment you stop treating it as 'just code' and start designing for team boundaries, the architecture chooses itself.”
