— The problem

Research labs were tracking specimens, structures, and locations in a spreadsheet sprawl. They needed one place that handled drag-and-drop inventory, in-app molecule drawing, geographic mapping of samples, undo/redo on every action, and rich-text notes — without ever losing data.

— Approach
  1. 01

    Integrated Ketcher for in-browser chemical structure drawing — chemists draw, the app stores canonical SMILES.

  2. 02

    Built a drag-and-drop inventory model on top of NgRx with full undo/redo via action replay — every change is reversible.

  3. 03

    Embedded Google Maps for sample provenance, with custom overlays for batches and collection sites.

  4. 04

    Designed a complete reusable component library and design system — the second half of the engagement was 4× faster than the first because the foundation was real.
